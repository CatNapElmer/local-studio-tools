Local Studio Tools

Local Studio Tools is a small personal program that runs on one home PC. The owner of the Local Studio YouTube channel uses it to manage that channel through the YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Lists the channel's own videos, playlists and view counts.
  • Edits the channel's own video titles, descriptions, tags and visibility.
  • Adds videos to and removes them from the channel's own playlists.
  • Reads comments on the channel's videos and posts replies as the channel.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or use it, and it only ever signs in to the owner's own Google account.
